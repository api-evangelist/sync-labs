# Sync

AI video lip-sync and visual dubbing platform.

Generate studio-grade lip-sync for films, ads, dubbing, and content creation via API or web studio. Founded in 2023, backed by Y Combinator (W24).

## Models

- **sync-3**: Advanced lip-sync model for side angles, low light, animation, and closed lips. $0.00534/frame at 25fps.
- **lipsync-2-pro**: Studio-grade lip-sync model. $0.00333/frame at 25fps.
- **react-1**: AI emotion editing for video expressions. $0.00667/frame at 25fps.
- **lipsync-2**: Standard lip-sync model. $0.002/frame at 25fps.
- **lipsync-1.9.0**: Legacy lip-sync model. $0.001/frame at 25fps.
